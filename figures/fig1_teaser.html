a · the instrument

Same question, two languages; same web, served three ways

Parallel Hindi/English and Telugu/English questions whose answer is verifiably in both Wikipedias. A search agent (3 searches, then answer) over a frozen pool served English-only, Indic-only, or both. The script of every query is logged under a free policy, then the policy is forced: match, english, dual.
query-side gap = what the agent's queries lose · evidence-side gap = what the web lacks · integration gap = what reading loses
